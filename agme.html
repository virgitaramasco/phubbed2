AGME Milano

AGME was born from the need to express moods and sensations captured in moments, in people, in what surrounds us or simply in the idea, the perception they leave us.

It comes from a small word, but with immense value. It's the root from which everything springs. It's the love we carry within us and that we feel the need to show the world. Digging deep within ourselves to understand our true path is one of the most difficult challenges.

We often live under the weight of other people's thoughts, of what is accepted or not.

But learning to take risks and turn your dreams into reality is the only way to feel alive. Dreaming helps us become who we truly are.
